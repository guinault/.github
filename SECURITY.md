# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you have identified a vulnerability affecting one of our services or
products, please email **a.bonamygreau@guinault.com**.

Please include: a description, the estimated impact, steps to reproduce,
and the affected service or version.

## Our commitments

| Stage | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of receipt | 3 working days |
| Initial assessment | 10 working days |
| Fix (high severity) | 30 days |

## Scope

Our products include aviation ground support equipment and their
monitoring systems. Any vulnerability affecting equipment control or
remote access is treated as high priority.

## Recognition

We do not run a paid bug bounty programme, but we are glad to credit
anyone who reports a vulnerability, unless they prefer otherwise.
